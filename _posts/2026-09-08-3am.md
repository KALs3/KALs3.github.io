but i woke up the next day losing the reason to do so

My Body is a machine 

i have been on a decline for a long period only going downhill been in a mental battle with myself and in this battle i lost .. a lot 
i can't let myself go back there anymore 
My Body is a machine that i need to keep busy 
My mind is a monster that is hungry for thoughts 
i need to keep both busy
My heart is a faint kid 
that i need to let go of 
i can't let it take control of me again 
My body is a machine

i need to get stronger , lift weights 
i need to get smarter , study more 
i need to feel less , stay busy
i need to express more , create more 

My body is a machine 
that has a brain that needs to be shut 
i no longer deserve or have the luxury to ruminate or overthink  
i no longer have the luxury of time and quality of life that gives me the permission 
to think beyond what i see, experience and sense no more assumptions no more thinking ahead of every possible scenario no more OVER thinking 

My body is a machine 
that if i don't use to my benefit it's gonna kill me 

My body is a machine 
That will help me achieve all the things i want and feel what i always longed for 

My heart needs to stop 
My heart is not a machine 
it's a delicate fragile relic i can't let it on display all the time 
My heart needs to stop 

My body is a machine 
from now onward just moves forward performs action gets stuff done and doesn't question anything 

My body is a machine 
that didn't find what it wanted 
or felt what it needed 
so it turned into a machine 

My body is a machine 
That will make me feel happy and get me what i want and help me feel everything i couldn't feel right now 

My body is a machine 
that will make it possible ...
