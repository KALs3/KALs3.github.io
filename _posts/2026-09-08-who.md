i feel like losing hope i feel like my life or our lives in general has become a rat manufacturing factory where we only live to serve one single purpose of trying to fit in and work towards getting a job we all consume the same content that shapes our minds we consume the same ideas we all share the same collective awareness

and its depressing to think that this is what life has come to be day after day only worrying about whether or not i will be able to find a job just to have some money to get food and go out do the most basic stuff and hopefully buy a house and get married all the basics have turned into a life long chase fueled by chronic stress and anxiety knowing that there are millions of people out there ready to take your place any moment you let loose for a second

at least i want to be in tune with myself i already lost who i am in that state of constant worrying and chasing i want to have my own ideas my own outlet

i want to immerse myself again in what is considered pure human experience rather than this manufactured superficial reality

but how do i find myself or at least the version i remember of myself

and to be honest i got no answer to that i am still in the search for who i am

i just came to the idea of consuming more meaningful stories art philosophy and more that talks to the soul rather than what tries to hijack your brain to generate more money to some corporate soulless company

i will try to document my pursuit

it wont be dramatic

i want to be in tune again with myself

and i dont want an audience for that

this is in pursuit of the lost me who was able to write document and feel himself before i lost him and became a machine
